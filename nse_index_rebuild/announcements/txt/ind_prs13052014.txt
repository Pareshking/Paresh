                                     PRESS RELEASE

                          Launch of CNX Shariah25 Index

In continuation of its efforts to develop indices that meet the requirements of market
participants, IISL announces launch of CNX Shariah25 index. The CNX Shariah25 index is
designed to provide investors exposure to the large shariah compliant stocks while making
the index easily replicable and tradable. The CNX Shariah25 Index comprises of 25 shariah
compliant companies listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).


The index will be calculated on an end-of-day basis. Index methodology & factsheet are
available at
http://www.nseindia.com/products/content/equities/indices/thematic_indices.htm
