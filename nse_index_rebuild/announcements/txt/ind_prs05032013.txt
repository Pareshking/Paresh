                                    PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Orient Paper & Industries Limited will be traded on an ex-entitlement basis
on National Stock Exchange with effect from March 7, 2013 on account of Scheme of
Arrangement involving the demerger of the Cement undertaking of Orient Paper &
Industries Limited and transfer and vesting into Orient Cement Limited.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following change in
CNX 500 index in this regard which will become effective from March 7, 2013.
