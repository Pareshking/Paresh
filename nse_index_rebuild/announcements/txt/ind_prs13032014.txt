                     ONLINE CALCULATION OF CPSE INDEX

In order to facilitate Government of India’s initiative to disinvest some of its stake in
selected Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) via ETF, IISL has constructed &
launched CPSE Index. The index has 10 constituent CPSEs. IISL is pleased to announce the
online calculation of CPSE Index effective from Tuesday, March 18, 2014.

Index methodology & factsheet are available at
http://www.nseindia.com/products/content/equities/indices/thematic_indices.htm
