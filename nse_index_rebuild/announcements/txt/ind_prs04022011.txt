                                       PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Himachal Futuristic Communications Ltd. (HFCL) will be suspended from trading on
the National Stock Exchange with effect from February 8, 2011 on account of Scheme of
Amalgamation of Sunvision Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. into HFCL.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following change in this regard
which will become effective from February 8, 2011:

1. S&P CNX 500 Index:

The following company is being excluded:

  Sr. No.                Company Name                    Symbol
     1      Himachal     Futuristic Communications    HIMACHLFUT
            Ltd.

The following company is being included:

  Sr. No.              Company Name                      Symbol
     1      Reliance MediaWorks Ltd.                    RELMEDIA

Place: Mumbai
