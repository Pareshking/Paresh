                                   PRESS RELEASE

                          Launch of LIX15 Midcap Index

In continuation of its efforts to develop indices that meet the requirements of market
participants, IISL announces launch of LIX15 Midcap Index. The LIX15 Midcap Index is
designed to provide investors exposure to liquid midcap stocks, making the index easily
replicable and tradable. The LIX15 Midcap Index comprises of 15 midcap companies listed
on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).


The index will be calculated on an end-of-day basis. Index methodology & factsheet are
available at
http://www.nseindia.com/products/content/equities/indices/LIX_15_Midcap.htm

Date: May 08, 2014
