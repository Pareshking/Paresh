                                    PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Television Eighteen India Limited will be suspended from trading on the National
Stock Exchange with effect from June 21, 2011 on account of the Scheme of Arrangement being
demerger of ‘News Business Undertaking’ of Television Eighteen India Limited into IBN 18
Broadcast Limited & merger of the demerged Television Eighteen India Limited into Network 18
Media & Investments Limited.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in various
indices in this regard which will become effective from June 21, 2011:


1) S&P CNX 500 Index

The following company is being excluded:

  Sr.                 Company Name                          Symbol
  No.
   1      Television Eighteen India Limited                  TV-18

The following company is being included:

  Sr.                 Company Name                          Symbol
  No.
   1     Indiabulls Real Estate Limited                  IBREALEST

2) CNX Smallcap Index

The following company is being excluded:
