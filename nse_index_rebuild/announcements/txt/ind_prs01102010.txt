                                        PRESS RELEASE

The ex-date for Zee Entertainment Enterprises Limited is October 7, 2010 on account of Composite
Scheme of Amalgamation and Arrangement (relating to Demerger of Education Business
Undertaking of Zee Entertainment Enterprises Limited and transfer/ vesting of the said undertaking
in favour of Zee Learn Limited).

Further, Reliance Natural Resources Limited has informed NSE that the Equity Shareholders of the
Company at their meeting held on September 4, 2010 as per the Order of the Honourable High
Court of Judicature at Mumbai have approved the Composite Scheme of Arrangement, inter alia,
between Reliance Natural Resources Limited and Reliance Power Limited.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in this regard
which will become effective from October 7, 2010:
