Replacements in Indices

50, NIFTY High Beta 50, NIFTY Low Volatility 50 and NIFTY100 Low Volatility 30 indices
as part of the quarterly review and decided to make the following replacements.
These changes shall become effective from September 28, 2018 (close of September
27, 2018).
