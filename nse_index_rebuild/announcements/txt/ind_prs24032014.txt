      LAUNCH OF “GSECBM NSE” INDEX ON 10 YEAR BENCHMARK G-SEC



IISL, in its continuous efforts to serve market participants with suitable financial benchmarks for
Indian capital market, has launched “GSECBM NSE” Index on 10 Year Benchmark G-Sec
effective March 24, 2014.

The total return index shall consider price movement of the benchmark security, as identified and
announced by FIMMDA, and accrued interest income of the security on daily basis.

The base date is January 03, 2011 and base value is 1000. The index is rebalanced from effective
date of change in the benchmark security.

The index will be maintained by IISL and calculated on an end-of-day basis. The closing values
of this index would be available on www.nseindia.com.
