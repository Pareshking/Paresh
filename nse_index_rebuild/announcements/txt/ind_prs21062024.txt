PRESS RELEASE
Mumbai, June 21, 2024



                        Replacement in Nifty SME Emerge index

      NSE Indices Limited had in its press release dated June 07, 2024 announced exclusion of Innovative
      Tyres and Tubes Ltd. (INNOVATIVE) effective June 28, 2024 (close of June 27, 2024) from Nifty
      SME Emerge index.

      NSE vide circular NSE/CML/62506 dated June 19, 2024 has announced suspension of Innovative Tyres
      and Tubes Ltd. (INNOVATIVE) effective June 26, 2024 (close of June 25, 2024).

      In view of the above, the Index Maintenance Sub-Committee (Equity) of NSE Indices Limited has
      decided to exclude the above-mentioned security from Nifty SME Emerge index effective June 25,
      2024 (close of June 24, 2024) instead of June 28, 2024.
