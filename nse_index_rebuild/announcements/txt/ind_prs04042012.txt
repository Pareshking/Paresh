                                   PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Zuari Industries Limited will be traded on an ex-entitlement basis on
National Stock Exchange with effect from April 9, 2012 on account of Scheme of
Arrangement involving the demerger of the Fertilizer undertaking of Zuari Industries
Limited into Zuari Holdings Limited.

Further, securities of Alfa Laval (India) Limited will be suspended for trading on
National Stock Exchange w.e.f. April 12, 2012 on account of voluntary delisting pursuant
to SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2009.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in
various indices in this regard as per the effective dates given below:

1) S&P CNX 500 Index

The following companies are being excluded:

 Sr. No.                Company Name                      Symbol           Effective Date
    1      Zuari Industries Limited                    ZUARIAGRO           April 9, 2012
    2      Alfa Laval (India) Limited                  ALFALAVAL           April 12, 2012

The following companies are being included:

 Sr. No.               Company Name                        Symbol          Effective Date
    1      United Breweries (Holdings) Limited         UBHOLDINGS          April 9, 2012
    2      J. Kumar Infraprojects Limited              JKIL                April 12, 2012
