       INDIA INDEX SERVICES & PRODUCTS LIMITED

                                  PRESS RELEASE

India Index Services & Products Ltd. (IISL) announces licensing of CNX Nifty index for
6th exchange traded fund (ETF) in international market.


Woori Asset Management on June 26, 2014 launched KOSEF Synthetic India ETF which
is benchmarked to CNX Nifty index. The ETF is listed on Korea Exchange.


This is the first India focused ETF launched in South Korea.
