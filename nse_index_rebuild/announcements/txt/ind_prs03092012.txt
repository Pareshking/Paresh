                                  PRESS RELEASE

Futures and Options contracts have not been introduced by National Stock Exchange of
India Limited vide its circular NSE/FAOP/21511 dated August 21, 2012 for NCC
Limited, Shipping Corporation Of India Limited and The South Indian Bank Limited.

In view of the above, the Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the
following changes in various indices which will become effective from September 28,
2012.

1) Nifty Midcap 50

The following companies are being excluded:

 Sr. No.               Company Name                       Symbol
    1      NCC limited                                NCC
    2      Shipping Corporation Of India Limited      SCI
    3      The South Indian Bank Limited              SOUTHBANK

Earlier, vide IISL press release dated August 16, 2012, The South Indian Bank Limited
was proposed to be included in Nifty Midcap 50 index w.e.f. September 28, 2012.

The following companies are being included:

 Sr. No.               Company Name                        Symbol
    1      Apollo Tyres Limited                       APOLLOTYRE
    2      Bhushan Steel Limited                      BHUSANSTL
    3      Dish TV India Limited                      DISHTV

2) CNX Infrastructure Index

The following company is being excluded:

 Sr. No.               Company Name                         Symbol
    1      Shipping Corporation Of India Limited      SCI

The following company is being included:

 Sr. No.             Company Name                         Symbol
    1      IVRCL Limited                              IVRCLINFRA
