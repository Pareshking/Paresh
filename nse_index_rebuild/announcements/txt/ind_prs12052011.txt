                                    PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Essar Shipping Ports & Logistics Limited will be suspended for trading on the
National Stock Exchange with effect from May 18, 2011 on account of the Scheme of
Arrangement being the amalgamation of Essar Ports & Terminals Limited and Essar International
Limited with the company and Demerger between Essar Shipping Ports & Logistics Limited
(Demerged Company) and Essar Shipping Limited (Resulting Company).

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in various
indices in this regard which will become effective from May 18, 2011:


1) S&P CNX 500 Index

The following company is being excluded:

  Sr.                 Company Name                         Symbol
  No.
   1      Essar Shipping Ports & Logistics Limited       ESSARSHIP

The following company is being included:

  Sr.                 Company Name                         Symbol
  No.
   1     ARSS Infrastructure Projects Limited            ARSSINFRA

2) CNX Smallcap Index

The following company is being excluded:

  Sr.                 Company Name                         Symbol
  No.
   1      Essar Shipping Ports & Logistics Limited       ESSARSHIP

The following company is being included:

  No.
