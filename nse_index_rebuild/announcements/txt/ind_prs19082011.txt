                                      PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Nagarjuna Fertilizer & Chemicals Limited (NFCL) will be suspended from trading
on the National Stock Exchange with effect from August 29, 2011 on account of the Scheme of
Arrangement and Amalgamation being demerger of the Oil Business Undertaking of NFCL into
Nagarjuna Oil Refinery Limited and merger of residual NFCL and iKisan Limited into Kakinada
Fertilizers Limited.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in various
indices in this regard which will become effective from August 29, 2011:


1) S&P CNX 500 Index

The following company is being excluded:

  No.
