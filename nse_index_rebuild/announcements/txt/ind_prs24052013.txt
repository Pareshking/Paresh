                   Change in Screening Partner for CNX Shariah Indices

IISL owns and manages CNX Nifty Shariah and CNX 500 Shariah indices. The indices are
calculated and disseminated on end-of-day basis and are tracked widely by the market
participants within India and outside India.

Effective May 27, 2013, Taqwaa Advisory and Shariah Investment Solutions (TASIS) would be
the screening the stock for Shariah compliance for CNX Nifty Shariah and CNX 500 Shariah
indices.

TASIS is India’s premier Shariah advisory institution in the field of business, finance, education
and research. Started in 2008, TASIS has since then been playing an important role in the Indian
financial landscape by structuring products to comply with Shariah, monitoring and certifying
businesses and corporates to assure investors and the general public about the quality of their
Shariah compliance.
