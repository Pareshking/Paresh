                                   PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Indiabulls Financial Limited will be suspended on National Stock Exchange
with effect from March 19, 2013 on account of Scheme of Arrangement for
amalgamation of Indiabulls Financial Services Limited (IBFSL) with Indiabulls Housing
Finance Limited (IHFL).


The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in
various indices in this regard to be effective March 19,2013.
