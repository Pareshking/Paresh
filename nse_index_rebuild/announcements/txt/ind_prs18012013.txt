Securities of Deccan Chronicle Holdings Limited will be suspended for trading on
National Stock Exchange w.e.f. January 23, 2013 on account of non-compliance with
provisions of Listing Agreement.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in
this regard in S&P CNX 500 and CNX Media index which will become effective from
January 23, 2013:


1) S&P CNX 500 Index

The following company is being excluded:

  No.

  No.
