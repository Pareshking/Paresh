                                    PRESS RELEASE

The ex-date for Zee News Limited is April 15, 2010 on account of the scheme of arrangement/
demerger between Zee News Limited and Zee Entertainment Enterprises Limited.
Further, securities of Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited will be suspended from trading on the
National Stock Exchange with effect from April 21, 2010 on account of the scheme of
arrangement for demerger.
The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes to S&P CNX
500 Index in this regard which will become effective from April 15, 2010:

The following companies are being excluded:

  Sr. No.   Company Name                             Symbol
     1      Zee News Limited                         ZEENEWS
     2      Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited            KIRLOSOIL

The following companies are being included:

  Sr. No. Company Name                               Symbol
     1    Adani Power Limited                        ADANIPOWER
     2    Oil India Limited                          OIL
