Futures and Options contracts have not been issued by National Stock Exchange of India
Limited for new expiry months for Bhushan Steel Limited and GVK Power and
Infrastructure Limited.

Nifty Midcap 50 Index in this regard which will become effective from March 1, 2013.

  No.

  Sr.                 Company Name                       Symbol
  No.

   2     Steel Authority of India Limited          SAIL
