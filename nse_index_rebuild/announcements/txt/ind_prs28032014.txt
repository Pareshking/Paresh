                                    PRESS RELEASE

                             LAUNCH OF NV20 INDEX

In continuation of its efforts to develop indices that meet the requirements of market
participants, IISL announces launch of NV20 Index. The NV20 Index is designed to reflect
the behaviour and performance of a diversified portfolio of value companies forming a part
of CNX Nifty Index selected on the basis of value parameters. The NV20 Index comprises
of 20 companies listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).


The index will be calculated on an end-of-day basis. The closing value and detailed
methodology of the index would be available on www.nseindia.com.
