                                   PRESS RELEASE

        Discontinuation of Real-Time Calculation of CNX Defty Index


Effective April 1, 2013 real-time calculation of CNX Defty Index would be discontinued.
The index would be calculated and disseminated daily on an end of day basis. The
closing values of the index would be available on www.nseindia.com.

Date: March 22, 2013
