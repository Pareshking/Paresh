                                   PRESS RELEASE

Securities of Future Retail Limited, formerly known as Pantaloon Retail (India) Limited
will be traded on an ex-entitlement basis on National Stock Exchange with effect from
April 17, 2013 on account of Scheme of Arrangement between Pantaloon Retail (India)
Limited (Demerged Company) and Peter England Fashions and Retail Limited
(Resulting Company).

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in
various indices in this regard which will become effective from April 17, 2013.
