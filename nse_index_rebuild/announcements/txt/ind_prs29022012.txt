                                  PRESS RELEASE

Securities of UTV Software Communications Limited and Carol Info Services Limited
will be suspended for trading on National Stock Exchange w.e.f. March 9, 2012 and
March 12, 2012 respectively on account of voluntary delisting pursuant to SEBI
(Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2009.

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in
various indices in this regard which will become effective from March 9, 2012:

1) S&P CNX 500 Index

The following companies are being excluded:

  No.
