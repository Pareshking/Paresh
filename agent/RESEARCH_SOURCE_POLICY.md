# Stage-4 Research Source Policy

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: Living policy

## Source hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Primary
- company investor-relations disclosures;
- NSE/BSE exchange filings;
- SEBI, RBI, MCA and other regulator/government disclosures;
- official results, investor presentations and earnings-call transcripts;
- official company press releases.

### Tier 2 — Secondary
- Reuters;
- established financial journalism;
- established financial-information providers and exchange-filing indexes used for discovery.

Secondary sources may identify an event, but a material company claim should be corroborated with a primary filing when reasonably available.

### Tier 3 — Derived
Calculations or transformations made from cited source material. Derived evidence must expose its inputs and must never be presented as an external fact.

## Rules

1. Never fabricate a citation.
2. Never treat a search result title as proof of an uninspected financial consequence.
3. Record event/publication date separately from retrieval date.
4. Never turn missing evidence into a negative conclusion.
5. Never treat a media report as a completed corporate event without corroboration when corroboration is available.
6. Preserve contradictory evidence.
7. Do not infer management motives.
8. Do not convert research evidence into a qualitative numerical score.
9. For historical research, enforce the information-available-as-of cutoff.
10. Current research must disclose the retrieval date.
11. If the underlying document is not reviewed, describe the evidence at the level actually verified and leave deeper implications UNKNOWN.
12. Corporate actions already owned by Paresh remain owned by the canonical corporate-action subsystem; Stage 4 only researches their qualitative significance.

## Evidence classification

POSITIVE means the documented fact supports the research question being examined.

NEGATIVE means the documented fact contradicts the research question or introduces a documented adverse consideration.

UNKNOWN means the fact is relevant but its directional effect cannot be established without further evidence. UNKNOWN is not a weak form of NEGATIVE.

## Publication gate

A report is publishable only when each substantive claim has a traceable source, dates are coherent, entity identity is correct, evidence classification is explicit, and unresolved uncertainty is visible.
