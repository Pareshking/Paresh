# Stage 4B second-archetype acceptance

This acceptance sample uses **ANANDRATHI (Anand Rathi Wealth Limited)**, rank 7 in
the 2026-09-18 canonical Top-25 snapshot, to demonstrate that adaptive research
does not turn the domain library into a universal checklist.

The research lens is built around AUM flows versus market appreciation, client
retention, relationship-manager productivity, recurring wealth/distribution
economics, employee-cost leverage, product/regulatory changes, digital/UK
channels and capital-market sensitivity.

Manufacturing capacity, raw materials, bank lending metrics and plant utilisation
are explicitly excluded because they are not material drivers of this business.

The packet uses real company, management and regulatory evidence reviewed on
2026-09-19. It is an acceptance artifact, not an investment recommendation.
