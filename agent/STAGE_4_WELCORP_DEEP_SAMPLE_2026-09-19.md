## 11. Tariff / trade-war scenario

This is especially important for WELCORP.

The company itself has stated that India-to-U.S. supply is not currently feasible under the prevailing tariff regime, making manufacturing location strategically relevant. This should be confirmed against the primary earnings-call transcript before being treated as a durable fact. urlCompany investor materialshttps://welspuncorp.com/financial-results.php

### Research questions

- What U.S. tariffs apply to relevant products?
- Does WELCORP's U.S.-manufactured output avoid the same tariff?
- Which competitors are India-export dependent?
- Are contracts fixed-price or pass-through?
- Who bears tariffs?
- Can customer pricing adjust?
- Does the tariff affect only new orders or existing contracts?
- Are there exemptions?

This can reveal **asymmetric peer exposure**.
