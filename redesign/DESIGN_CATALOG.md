## Mockup naming convention

`Dxx_<surface>_<viewport>_vN`

Examples:

- `D02_screener_desktop_v1`
- `D02_screener_mobile_v1`
- `D03_stock_detail_desktop_v1`
- `D03_stock_detail_mobile_v1`

## Important

Generated mockups are design references. Any numbers shown in a mockup are illustrative unless explicitly marked as production data. They must not be copied into application logic as hard-coded values.

The catalog is updated whenever a design is created, revised, accepted, or superseded.
