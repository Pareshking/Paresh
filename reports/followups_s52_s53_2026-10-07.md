Cause: on Screener's weekly stretch (before about 26 Sep 2025) Screener's close for
these stocks **is BSE's close**: 34 of 34 weekly dates, Sep 2024 - Apr 2025, equal BSE's
raw close (times the long file's factor) within 0.05%, and NSE's on 2 - 5 of 34
(the days the two exchanges closed the same). The four weeks are those where the two
exchanges' closes sat 1 - 6% apart at an end (PTCIL in NSE's BE series). Not a split or
bonus timing, and not a Screener error in the price it shows; it is the other
exchange. Tijori matches Screener on all of them, so Tijori is BSE too there: its
agreement is not a second vote.

How common (`b_screener_exchange_weekly_stretch.csv`; days the two exchanges' closes
differ by 0.1% or more, stock mapped to one BSE code by ISIN): weekly stretch 1 Sep 2024 -
20 Sep 2025, 923 stocks: **74 BSE-priced** (TITAN, IRCTC, TORNTPHARM, SHRIRAMFIN,
ETERNAL, NYKAA, PTCIL, SKYGOLD, SHAILY, MANORAMA, LLOYDSENGG, LLOYDSENT ...), 818 NSE,
0 mixed. Daily stretch 1 Oct 2025 - 30 Sep 2026, 980 stocks: 3 BSE (SHILCTECH, TIMEX,
TVSHLTD; the first two traded on BSE before NSE), 977 NSE. So the weekly fill splices
NSE's daily moves onto BSE weekly anchors for 74 stocks; the 2% check refuses only the
weeks where the two exchanges sat far apart. Not checked: Screener's own documentation
of which exchange it uses; why it switched to NSE on its daily stretch.
