## Faults found

### One action applied twice: a day-and-month swap inside the listing window

NSE's daily Bc files carry numeric dates; some rows stored before 27 Sep 2026 read them day first. `repair_swapped_dates` swaps a date back only when it lies far outside the window around the file's day. Two swapped dates fell inside it, so the build saw the action twice:

| Stock | Right date | Second date (swapped) | What it did |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALLCARGO | Demerger, ex 12 Nov 2025 (factor 0.3508) | 11 Dec 2025 | That day's -3.6% priced as a second demerger (x0.964) |
| HCG | Rights 1:17 at Rs 512, record date 2 Mar 2026 ([HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/healthcare-global-enterprises-sets-rs-512-rights-issue-price-record-date-march-2-2026); terms set 24 Feb, [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HCG1_24022026195648_Board_Meeting_Outcome_-_24_February_2026.pdf)) | 3 Feb 2026 | Applied again a month earlier (x0.9928) |

Splits and bonuses are safe from this (the price must confirm them); rights issues, demergers and dividends were not. A scan of every step in the file for pairs at swapped dates finds only these two. Neither stock was a Nifty 500 member then; both were in Total Market and Microcap 250, so History runs on those indices read them 3.6% and 0.7% off until the rebuild.

Fix: `nse_bundle.drop_swapped_twins`. The yearly list spells the month, so for the same stock and kind its date wins over a Bc row at the swapped date; `read_actions` now tags each row's list. The long file must be rebuilt after the merge (workflow `nse_long_prices.yml`) and the two steps checked gone.

### S28: benchmarks.csv lost its trailing zeros

On pandas 3 (the version the sync installs), `benchmark_store.merge` with an empty frame of new rows (a day NSE gave nothing) makes the closes `object`, and `to_csv(float_format="%.2f")` skips them: 19384.30 was committed as 19384.3. Pandas 2.3 keeps them float, which is why it did not show on the laptop. Fix: `merge` ignores empty frames and `write` casts the closes to float. The next daily sync rewrites the file with two decimals on every row (formatting only; no value changes).

## S8: every month of the History backtest against the rules (added 7 Oct, evening)

The owner asked for every month's trades to be checked against our rules, against the price history and against the exits; Tijori once for the backtest differences.

**Rules, recomputed independently.** For each of the 201 rebalances (Jan 2010 - Sep 2026), the book was rebuilt from the definitions alone (no engine function called):
- **Filters on the signal date (the last session of the month):**
  - Nifty 500 member, from the point-in-time timeline.
  - Close above its 50-session EMA.
  - Close at or above 80% of its 252-session high.
  - 20-session average traded value of at least Rs 5 Cr.
- **Score:** the composite of the 1, 3, 6, 9 and 12-month period Sharpe, each winsorised and z-scored across the frame (weights 0.10 / 0.30 / 0.30 / 0.20 / 0.10).
- **Selection:**
  - Holdings are kept while they rank inside the top 40; the book is then topped up to 20 in rank order.
  - At most 8 names per industry (40% at 5% each), and one share line per company.
- **Returns:** equal weight held from fill to fill; the turnover cost is 30 bps.

| Check (201 months) | Result |
|---|---|
| Fill on the first session of the month | 201 |
| Independent book = engine's book, given the engine's previous book | 201 |
| Independent book = engine's book, chained over the whole run | 201 |
| Months with a rule broken (a holding not a member, failing a filter, kept beyond rank 40, an industry over 8, two share lines) or an exit whose stated reason is false | 0 |
| Monthly return by hand vs the engine's (net + cost) | largest gap 0.042 pts |
| Nifty 500 monthly return by hand vs the engine's | exact |

**Every trade against independent prices** (`s8_trades.csv`): 1,590 closed trades; 1,587 have a reference over the same two dates.
- 1,583 are within 2 points of at least one reference, and 1,567 of all the references compared.
- The 4 outside are adjustments the references do not make:
  - dividends of 10% or more (PFIZER Dec 2013, HINDZINC 2016, IDFC 2023);
  - CASTROL's 2010 1:1 bonus, which MarketLens leaves raw.
- **Tijori**, over the 1,260 trades it covers: 1,258 within 2 points, median gap 0.0; summed trade returns 64.27 on ours against 63.84 on Tijori's.

**Stale fills:** none at entry. Three exits were struck on a carried price: UTVSOF, ALFALAVAL and PATNI (2012), the takeovers of S27 whose holders were bought out near the last price.

**TUBEINVEST (S26), checked again after an outside review.** NSE Indices' press release of 27 Apr 2017 (`ind_prs27042017.txt`) excluded Tube Investments from the Nifty 500 and two other index lists "on account of scheme of arrangements… Demerger of Manufacturing Business undertaking", effective 26 May 2017. Our timeline has the same date, three months before the demerger (Aug 2017). The only TUBEINVEST trade in the run is June 2010. The general risk the review raised, a stock held through a demerger, is covered two ways:
- a stock that leaves the index is sold at the next rebalance ("Not in the index");
- a demerger in the long file is priced at the ex-date fall, so the holder's value carries into the successor's series rather than reading as a crash.
