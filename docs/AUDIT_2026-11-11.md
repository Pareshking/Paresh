## Q-H2: no uncertainty shown, and almost no evidence yet (Owner, S122)

Across the 10 months the monthly alpha has mean +4.4 pts and standard deviation 6.2 pts:
a tracking error of about **21% a year**. Nine of those months are in-sample, so this
measures how noisy the record is, not whether there is skill. At that tracking error,
the years of recorded months a true alpha needs before it reads at t = 2 are
(2 × 0.21 / alpha)²:

| True alpha a year | Years to t = 2 |
|---|---|
| 5 pts | ~71 |
| 10 pts | ~18 |
| 20 pts | ~4.4 |

So the record cannot confirm a realistic edge for many years. It can still do two useful
things sooner: catch a broken implementation (live drifting from the replay), and check
that the strategy behaves as the backtest says (turnover, drawdown, beta, hit rate). The
Recommendation's shadow record review (TRACK_RECORD §8) already reasons this way.

Recommendation: next to "Ahead of the index", show the recorded months' alpha with a
90% interval, or the words "too short to judge" until 24 recorded months exist. Owner
decides the wording.

## Q-M1: costs (Partly done, S123)

`cost_bps = 30` per round trip, charged on one-way turnover (`backtester._step_portfolio_allocation`),
the same for the 750, Nano Cap and Combined. The Guide says it covers brokerage, fees,
GST, stamp duty, STT **and the bid-ask spread**. Delivery charges as I understand them
(I have not checked these against a dated circular in this session):

| Charge | Round trip, bps |
|---|---|
| STT 0.1% on buy and 0.1% on sell | 20.0 |
| Stamp duty 0.015% on the buy | 1.5 |
| NSE transaction charge, about 0.003% each side | ~0.6 |
| SEBI fee, GST on the above, DP charge on a ₹5 lakh sale | ~0.2 |
| **Statutory total** | **~22** |

That leaves about 8 bps for two half-spreads plus impact. That is plausible for the Nifty
100. It is probably not enough for the Microcap 250 slice of the 750, or for Nano Cap.
This has not been measured: no spread or impact figure exists anywhere in the repository.
At the model book's ₹1 crore (₹5 lakh a name) impact is small. The liquidity floor is off
in the pinned configuration (`liquidity.py`), so the figure would understate costs for
anyone running larger money.

Done when: (1) the statutory rates are checked against NSE's and SEBI's current
circulars and quoted with dates; (2) a spread estimate exists per size bucket, for example
Corwin–Schultz from NSE bhavcopy high/low, or order-book snapshots of the current holdings;
(3) the History backtest is reported at 30, 50 and 75 bps beside each other.

## Q-M2: tax against the alternative (Done, S124)

`scripts/research/rebalance_rules_after_tax.py` taxes the strategy (STCG 20.8%, LTCG 13%
over the ₹1.25 lakh exemption), giving 18.67% → 15.30% a year for the six-monthly reset.
It does not tax the benchmark. An investor's real alternative is a Nifty 500 index fund,
which pays LTCG only on exit, so its tax is deferred. The tax drag (about 3.4 pts a year)
falls almost entirely on the strategy, so the after-tax lead over an index fund is
smaller than the pre-tax lead by most of those 3.4 pts. The exact figure has not been
computed.

Recommendation: add the index held for the whole window, taxed once at the end, to that
script, and quote both after-tax figures together in the Guide.

15% hold cap, 5% buys. Nifty 500, point in time, 4 Jan 2010 to 30 Oct 2026:

| Cost, bps a round trip | Strategy before tax | Strategy after tax, sold at end | Nifty 500 before tax | Nifty 500 after tax, sold at end | Lead before tax | Lead after tax |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 18.56% | 15.14% | 10.06% | 9.35% | 8.50 pts | 5.79 pts |
| 50 | 17.52% | 14.10% | 10.06% | 9.35% | 7.46 pts | 4.75 pts |
| 75 | 16.23% | 12.80% | 10.06% | 9.35% | 6.17 pts | 3.45 pts |

- **S124:** tax takes 2.7 points a year off the lead (8.5 → 5.8). The index's gain is
  long-term and taxed once at the end, so it loses only 0.7 a year; the strategy loses 3.4.
- **S123:** each extra 20 bps a round trip costs about 1.0 point a year, consistent with
  about 5x a year of one-way turnover. At 75 bps and after tax, the lead is 3.5 points a year.
- Both sides are price only, at today's tax rates for every year, with no costs or expense
  ratio on the index position (this flatters the index slightly). Not checked against an
  independent source: the replay is our own engine.
- The 2026 window (750) was also run, but a 10-month figure is not annualised here (the
  site's own rule), so it is not quoted.

## Q-L3: dividends (Open, S125)

Both sides are price-only, which is consistent. Two things are not measured:
(a) a dividend of 10% of the price or more is taken out of the strategy's prices
(`nse_adjusted.LARGE_DIVIDEND`), so the strategy keeps those dividends while the index
does not; (b) momentum books usually yield less than the index, so a price-only comparison
flatters the strategy by the yield gap compared with a total-return comparison. Done
when the book's trailing dividend yield is set against the Nifty 500's for 2010–26 and
the gap is quoted.

## What holds up (checked, not assumed)

  Sharpe and a paired block bootstrap, and they conclude "no setting reliably beats the
  live one" rather than adopting the in-sample winner
  (`LOOKBACK_WEIGHTS_2026-10-12.md`, `REGIME_SETTINGS_2026-10-12.md`).
- The Recommendation is recorded forward as a shadow, not switched in on its backtest.
- A record under a year is not annualised (`track_record_view`, owner 7 Oct).
- Backtests from 2010 pick from point-in-time membership with former members priced. The
  Backtest page says how many rebalances fell back to today's list.
