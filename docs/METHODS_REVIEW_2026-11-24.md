## Summary

- **30 rows.** 10 Same (source quoted or decided) · 3 Different (fix) · 11 Different (keep, with reason) · 6 Not checked.
  The 6 Not checked rows are methods that look standard, but no source was fetched for them. Several "keep" rows
  still carry a wording fix (16, 19, 22).
- **Top 3 differences, ranked by how much a reader sees them:**
  1. **The momentum score skips no month, and its volatility is measured over each window's own sessions.**
     NSE's Nifty200 Momentum 30 and MSCI Momentum both measure to one month before the rebalance (M-1), and they scale by a
     fixed volatility: 1 year of daily log returns for NSE, 3 years of weekly returns for MSCI. Ours scores 1, 3, 6, 9
     and 12 months up to the latest close, each divided by its own window's volatility. This affects every published rank.
     Kept: the owner's signal, with skip-5 already run as a shadow book (S160). **Owner decision**; this review recommends
     no change, only saying so on the Guide.
  2. **The 52W high is read on closes. NSE reads it on intraday highs.** Measured: NSE's own 52-week file, effective
     20 Nov 2026, lists 66 stocks whose high was struck on 19 Nov. On 54 of them the day's high differed from the close,
     and NSE's figure equalled the high on all 54 (the close on none). So our "From 52W high" and "Near 52W high" differ from
     what a reader sees on NSE's quote page. Kept by the owner's decision (10 Nov). Fix the wording only:
     "52W closing high".
  3. **Dividend basis.** NSE's price indices are not free of dividends. NSE's methodology (Nov 2022) takes every
     dividend of 2% of the price or more out of the price index. Ours takes out only dividends of 10% or more. Dividends of
     2-10% are therefore neutral in the Nifty 500 but show as falls in our stocks. The Backtest note says the Nifty 500 has
     "no dividends", which is not quite right. The effect on the backtest's yearly return was not measured: between 0 and
     about 0.5 pts a year against the book (the book's whole dividend yield is about 0.5%, S125). Fixing the note's wording
     changes nothing shown. Changing the threshold would move the backtest and possibly ranks: **owner decision**.
- **Also found:** Portfolio annualises volatility and tracking error with √250 (`portfolio_report.py:27`). Everywhere
  else uses √252, as decided. Fixing it moves the Portfolio risk figures by 0.4%, for example 20.0% to 20.1%. It touches no
  ranks and no frozen months.
- **Not checked:**
  - niftyindices.com did not answer from this machine on 24 Nov: every request timed out or got an empty reply. NSE's
    Momentum 30 rules were therefore read from the Nov 2022 methodology on nsearchives instead.
  - The size of the 2-10% dividend effect.
  - The beta, tracking-error, Calmar and turnover conventions: no source was fetched.
  - The cost of using a fixed 6.5% risk-free rate.
