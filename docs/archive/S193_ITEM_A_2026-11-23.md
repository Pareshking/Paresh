## What was not checked
- **Watchlist with SANSERA.** A fresh browser's watchlist is empty ("Your watchlist is empty"). Adding SANSERA means saving to the site's browser storage, and the permission policy refused that step, so the Watchlist copies of rank, close, EMA and so on were not compared. From the code, the Watchlist takes its qualified trail from the same `rank_trails` call on `deep_adj_close` (`app.py:1211-1216`), so it should match Actions and Screener, not Portfolio (#4). That was not observed.
- **Backtest** figures were not compared with the other pages: it runs its own Nifty 500 history to 30 Oct. Its "Nifty 500 −10.6% below its peak" is to 30 Oct and from the all-time peak; it is not the 12-month high on Market and Indices.
- Values that appear only in charts, hover tooltips or the "Show the table" expanders were not read, apart from the chart text Streamlit exposes.
- Screener rows under the 1000 and nano were spot-checked only through the SANSERA stock page and the holdings' rank trails.
- The build the site served: no chip is visible (see the top of this report).
- No independent source: this is the site against itself only, with no NSE or BSE file checked.
- None of the causes above was reproduced by running code. They come from reading the repo at `dc57db5`, which may not be the commit the site ran.
