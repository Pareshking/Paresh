## How scored

Seven criteria, 1-10 each, read from the screenshots (my reading, not a measured score; a second reader would
move some by a point):

1. **First answer**: does the first screen say the answer in one short headline and a lede of two sentences?
2. **Charts**: a familiar form (a line over time, a bar), history long enough to judge "usual", labelled extremes.
3. **Calm**: one colour pair for up and down, colour on figures not on sentences, white space, few rules.
4. **Words**: short sentences; a question a reader would ask; no house terms unexplained.
5. **Sources**: a source line under each chart, its data a click away (table, CSV).
6. **Phone**: the first screen at 390 px reads as a page, with nothing over it.
7. **Consistency**: one grid, one type scale and one table style across pages.
