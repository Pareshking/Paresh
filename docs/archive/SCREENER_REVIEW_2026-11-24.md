### Considered and left out

- **Speed.** Nothing to fix, so no item. On a warm cache the heading appears in 0.6-1.1 s and the table's rows in 2.2-2.4 s. A preset rerun takes 2.6 s and a header sort 0.6 s. A stock page opens in 1.7 s, and Back brings back the same preset (`?view=pass` checked, `probe5`).
- **Saved screens** (screener.in's "Save Your Query", [guide, read 24 Nov 2026](https://www.screener.in/guides/creating-screens/); Tickertape's Save, [screener, read 24 Nov 2026](https://www.tickertape.in/screener); Finviz presets). The address already keeps every filter (`_sync_url`), so a bookmark is a saved screen. A save button adds little.
- **Big movers' threshold.** It selects 251 of 750 today, and the owner kept 150 places on 21 Nov (decision C), so it is not raised again.
- **Showing the compact Core list by default on a phone.** It would contradict requirement 4 (owner, 7 Nov); items 3 to 5 get most of the gain without changing it.
