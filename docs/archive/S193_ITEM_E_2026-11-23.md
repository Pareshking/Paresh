## 5. Proposed rules (one wording per cause, one form per state)

**R1. Missing value: "–" and one reason, beside it or in the table's key, worded by cause.** Five causes, five fixed phrases (template, S203):
- System too young: "– until the book has {n} sessions (it has {k})" / "before {system}'s first month, {Mon YYYY}".
  - Portfolio: "Beta – until the book has 60 sessions (it has 14)".
  - Dashboard 1000: "The book · October –" with the footnote "– before the Nifty 1000's first month, Nov 2026".
- Stock or index too new: "– listed {d Mon YYYY}, {n} sessions".
  - Screener: "12M return –", key "– listed 12 Aug 2026, 70 sessions".
  - Indices and Sectors: "since 28 Jan 2026" (one date form, with the year).
- Not published yet: "{name} to {d Mon}: NSE's {d Mon} close is not in yet".
  - Portfolio already says this.
  - The Dashboard, Market and Indices take the same sentence.
- No such index: "no NSE index for {group}" in the row label.
  - Dashboard: "Micro Cap · no momentum index".
  - Portfolio list: "Mix in the book's size split · no NSE index for Nano Cap".
- Failed the filters: **"Out"**, never "–".
  - LENSKART "#17 Out #18".
  - Screener movers "Rank 19 Oct: Out".

**R2. Not applicable is a blank cell, not "–".**
- Sectors "P/E" on the equal-weighted rows.
- Portfolio "Nifty Total Market · Since it started".
- Backtest "Nifty 500 · Turnover".

**R3. Every figure ends on the page's session (S148, S211), or the page says which date it ends on, in one sentence.**
- The sentence: "{source} to {d Mon}: {reason}".
- The footer "Data to" reads the session the header pill shows (`ranking_as_of`), not the frame's last row.
- Examples:
  - FPI tile "Foreign, 1 to 20 Nov";
  - Indices drops a 1M figure that does not end on 20 Nov;
  - the deferred session gives footer "Data to 19 Nov 2026 · NSE", matching the pill.

**R4. One form per state:**
- **Page has no data:** h1 in the page's voice ("No prices yet." / "No index data yet."), one lede naming what is missing (no job or workflow names), and the footer kept.
  - Sectors, Indices, Backtest, Actions and Portfolio would all take this form.
- **Section has no data:** h2 sentence plus one note line, in place of the section; never a silent gap.
  - Dashboard "4 · Money flows: NSDL's file is not in."
  - Market "No answer today: the Nifty 500 has no close for 20 Nov."
- **Filter matches nothing:** a `kit.empty` line naming the filter and the way out, never `st.info`.
  - Screener "No stock matches these filters. Clear the search or pick another preset."
  - Market "No new high in the last 20 sessions."
- **Something failed:** a `kit.note` in amber, saying what the reader can still trust. No exception text, no emoji, no internal names.
  - "The model book could not be read; the figures above are from 20 Nov."

**R5. One wording per repeated state:**
- "No model book yet: it forms once {system} has {n} months of prices." on the Dashboard, Actions and Portfolio.
- "No board meeting or ex-date is announced for {these stocks} before {d Mon}." on Actions and Watchlist.

**R6. Stale banner: one message, one date form.**
- The header banner (`stale_prices_notice`) is the only place that says prices are late.
- The pill always keeps the prices date, and another stale source is added after it ("Prices · closes of 20 Nov · market caps undated").
- The Screener foot stops restating it.
