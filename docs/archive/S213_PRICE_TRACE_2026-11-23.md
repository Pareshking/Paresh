## Findings

1. **CCAVENUE: an action NSE listed in words the parser did not read. Fixed by rule.**
   NSE's Bc file of 12 and 13 Oct lists it as `CNSLDATNRE1 TO RS10`, with record date 13 Oct and no
   ex-date. Evidence independent of our numbers: BSE's bhavcopy, scrip 539807, ISIN INE483S01020 to
   INE483S01038 on 19 Oct (same issuer, new face value), 13.81 on 12 Oct and 130.03 on 19 Oct; NSE's
   equity list, face value Rs 10.
   - Cause (1): `nse_bundle._CONSOLIDATION` matched only `CONSOLIDAT`, and `_FACE` needs a word
     boundary before "RE1".
   - Cause (2): every reader drops an action with no ex-date.
   - Rule: `CNSLDAT` is read as a consolidation, the face value is read when glued to the word, and
     `nse_bundle.fill_ex_dates` gives a split, consolidation or bonus with no ex-date its record date
     (under T+1 the two are the same day). `action_factors` then confirms the step on the first
     session the stock trades (19 Oct, x9.40 against 10) and applies it.
   - Reach across the whole 2008-2026 pack: four consolidations NSE abbreviates this way (KAUSHALYA,
     SHEKHAWATI, VERTOZ, CCAVENUE) and seven steps with no ex-date. In the long file only CCAVENUE
     changes: POWERMECH's 2024 bonus is the same row twice (removed by "one action, once"), and
     NANDAN has no price after its 2012 record date.
   - Effect: the live ranking reads Screener, which had it right, so no page showed it. The long
     file's readers are corrected at the next nightly build: Backtest history and the list's
     momentum record. The October row was unaffected, since CCAVENUE was not on the September
     month-end list; it would have counted from November.
   - Check: `scripts/check_long_against_screener.py`, every night in `nse_long_prices.yml`. A 50%+
     one-day move in one of the long file and the live Screener store but not the other fails the
     job. The Saturday audits compared against Screener's frozen 3 Oct copy, so they could not see
     it. Tests: `tests/test_s213_unlisted_consolidation.py`.
2. **TRIVENIPT: the app's frame started two sessions after the listing. Fixed by rule (S218, owner 23 Nov: "from now on").**
   Screener's first price is 14 Oct. `fill_from_backup` fills only after a stock's first Screener
   price ("before it, there is nothing to chain from"). NSE has both days, and for a new listing
   there is something to chain from: its own first closes, with no action yet to change the basis.
   The two lost closes include the highest so far (684.00 against 617.35). From its 126th session,
   the 52W-high test would read it nearer its high than it is, until those days leave the year.
   A rule would chain NSE's closes backwards from Screener's first close for a stock whose Screener
   history starts within a few sessions of its listing. It changes the ranking frame, so the owner
   decides whether it applies from a date forward (TRIVENIPT, and every listing after) or to the
   2025 listings too, which can move replays of frozen months.
3. **Explained, not faults** (rows in `docs/DATA_CORRECTNESS.md` section 5):
   - **SWANDEF**: trades once a week, on both exchanges.
   - **FMGOETZE**: a Rs 94 interim and special dividend (`INTDVSPDVRS 7.50 & 86.50`). The long file
     takes out dividends of 10% or more: 501.45 / (501.45 + 94) = 0.8421. Screener leaves them in.
   - **The two-year NSE closes end on 5 Nov.** They are published monthly by design, and fill only
     what Screener lacks. On 20 Nov every stock in the three universes had Screener's close
     (750, 1,000 and 411), so nothing was filled from the older copy.
