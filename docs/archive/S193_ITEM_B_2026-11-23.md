# S193 item B: every sentence stating a number or a claim, true to the data (23 Nov 2026)

**Method.** The app ran locally on main (2c91ca2), with prices of 20 Nov 2026.
- **What was read:** ten pages, each for the 750, the 1000 and the > ₹2,000 Cr list, at 1400 px.
- **What was kept:** every prose line of eight words or more that states a number or a claim (ahead, behind, most, every, only and so on). That gives 391 distinct sentence patterns.
- **How each was judged:** each pattern was traced to the code that writes it. Its figure was then recomputed with pandas from the raw data: NSE's daily long file, Screener's store, the index files, the ledgers and the membership lists. The verdict comes from that recomputation, not from the page's own function.
- **Who checked:** four agents in parallel. Two faults from each group were then re-checked by hand against the code, and all eight held.

Full tables, with evidence and code lines for every sentence: `S193_ITEM_B/b_g{1..4}_findings.md`. The groups are g1 Dashboard; g2 Portfolio, Actions, Screener and the stock page; g3 Sectors and Market; g4 Indices, Backtest and the Guide.

## 3. Wrong number from a sparse price history (one cause, a design choice)

Before Jul 2025, Screener's store holds about ten rows a month. Readings a year back or more taken from it are not what they say:
- **Market, 1000 and list:**
  - "54% … a year ago": NSE's daily closes give 47.2%.
  - "The usual range −4.3 to +19.3": NSE gives −2.3 to +15.3.
  - "New 52-week high in the last five sessions": five rows on the sparse stretch are two to three weeks.
- **Stock page:**
  - The 50D EMA: ₹1,448 shown, ₹1,454 on NSE.
  - RELIANCE's "all-time closing high ₹1,597 on 12 Jul 2024": NSE has ₹1,600.90 on 8 Jul 2024.
  - "From the long record": the figure comes from that Screener snapshot, not the long record.
- **Actions swap evidence:** "90 buys +3.1 pts": NSE closes give 91 buys, +2.7 pts. JBCHEPHARM is dropped without a word.

The ranking frame is filled with NSE's daily path (S23); these pages read the unfilled Screener frame. **Recommendation:** read history from the NSE-filled frame, or from the long file, and count windows in sessions or calendar days rather than rows. Opposite view: the filled frame costs memory on pages that don't need it now, and a year-ago reading is context, not a signal.
