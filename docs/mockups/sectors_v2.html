Which sectors lead

Lead: the sector’s return less the 750’s over the same sessions, both equal-weighted, in pts. Each line is 1 pt; the triangle marks the six-month lead read a month ago.

What it is · how to read it
Broad or narrow

Each sector’s ranked stocks, equal-weighted, against NSE’s index for the sector, which holds its own selection of the sector’s companies and weights them by free-float value: the two differ in stocks and in weights. Bars: the gap over three months, 0.5 pt a line. The dashed line is the 750’s own gap to Nifty Total Market: a bar left of it means the sector’s largest companies did more of the work than in the market as a whole.

One year, each line 100 a year ago (or at the index’s launch)

Month by month

Each cell is the sector’s lead over the 750 in that calendar month, both equal-weighted, in pts; the last month column is November so far. Run counts completed months in a row on the same side. The last column adds the twelve completed months.

Sector leadership map

Across: the sector’s lead over the 750 over the month, both equal-weighted, in pts (section 1’s 1M lead). Up: the share of its stocks above the EMA. The hollow dot is a month ago, the filled dot today. The leader is blue, the laggard rust, the rest grey; pick one to pick it out.

Show the table

The map’s figures are the live page’s (sector_breadth.sector_leadership), with five or more stocks per sector, so it counts the same 19 sectors as the rest of the page. On the built page the EMA (20, 50, 100, 200 days) and the period (1M, 3M) are switches; here the default is drawn.

Inside each sector

The share of each sector’s ranked stocks above their 200D EMA (exponential average of 200 sessions), 2% a line; the dashed lines are 40% and 60%, below which a rise is narrow and above which it is broad; the triangle marks a month ago. Then the ranking table’s own flags. Today’s members throughout, so older readings leave out stocks that have since left the 750.
