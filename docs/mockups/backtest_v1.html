Weekly closes. Blue: this rule; grey: Nifty 500; the figure after each is how far below its peak. Move the pointer, tap, or use the arrow keys. The deepest day, 23 Mar 2026, was −38.8%.

Price returns, both sides. The rule’s figure includes dividends only where one was 10% of the price or more (the long file takes those out as an adjustment); the Nifty 500 figure is NSE’s price index, with no dividends. NSE’s own file puts the Nifty 500’s dividend yield at 1.25% a year on average since Feb 2012, against about 0.5% for the book (S125, 2020–25). So against the index with dividends the lead is about 0.8 points a year smaller than shown.

A year, compounded is measured over calendar time, 16.8 years. The current page divides by 252 sessions a year; NSE averaged about 248, which raised the figure to 18.87% (the Guide quotes 18.56% for the same run).

Why the Nifty 500 and not Nifty Total Market. Total Market starts on 14 Oct 2021; this history starts in 2010 (owner, 21 Nov 2026, S165). Total Market is beside it from 2022 in section 3 and in section 4.

The two halves: the rule led by 0.85 pts a month on average to Jun 2018 and 0.52 after. Median month +0.92 pts.
