NSE INDEX MEMBERSHIP REBUILD - STATUS SUMMARY
================================================================================

Date: 2026-10-02 (supersedes the earlier "phase 1" summary)

WHAT EXISTS
  data/membership_history.json holds point-in-time membership for seven indices,
  rebuilt announcement by announcement from NSE press releases, backward from
  NSE's current lists:
    Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50, Nifty 500 ........ from 2010-01-01
    Nifty Midcap 150, Nifty Smallcap 250 ...... from 2016-04-01 (launch)
    Nifty Microcap 250 ........................ from 2021-09-30
    Nifty Total Market ........................ from 2021-10-29
  It also carries the 105 ticker and name changes found on the way
  (symbol_changes), the hand-made rules applied, and an (empty) caveats list.
  The daily sync keeps appending changes after 2026-09-30.

HOW IT WAS CHECKED
  - The chain of announcements closes at exact sizes on every date (50/50/150/
    250/250/750/500) with zero unresolved events.
  - The history file equals the reconstruction on all 384 end-of-day states.
  - NSE's own IndexInclExcl.xls agrees event by event for Nifty 50 (76 of 76),
    Next 50 (213 of 214) and Nifty 500 (1,078, no date difference), 2010-2020;
    38 differences remain, each explained.
  - Today's lists on two NSE hosts equal the history's current members (daily).
  - An independent AI reader re-derived 22 random notices (1,126 events) for the
    four indices the workbook does not cover: zero differences.
  - A frozen hash of the history through 2026-09-30 is checked in CI.

STATUS
  Adopted for production use by the repository owner on 2026-10-02. It is a
  reconstruction from NSE press releases cross-checked against NSE's workbook,
  not an NSE-certified record. Not met: an outside constituent list for Midcap
  150, Smallcap 250, Microcap 250 and Total Market before today; a person
  re-deriving a sample; compliance sign-off for regulatory use (not something
  this repository supplies).
